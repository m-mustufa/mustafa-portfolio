      What gets measured

      Built around the first client scan

      1. Relevance: does the proposal reflect the actual job instead of a reusable template?
      2. Opening: do the first lines lead with the client's problem rather than your biography?
      3. Proof: are there concrete tools, outcomes, numbers or examples?
      4. Clarity: is the proposal easy to scan without becoming vague or bloated?
      5. Next step: does it end with a useful question or a clear action?

      Questions

      Before you paste anything

      Does this tool store my Upwork proposal?

      No. This version runs entirely in your browser. The job post and proposal are not sent to a server.

      Does a high score guarantee a reply?

      No. It measures useful writing signals, not the client's final decision. Fit, timing, competition and your profile still matter.

      Should I copy the suggested opening word for word?

      Use it as a direction. Add a truthful example from your own work and keep the final wording natural.
