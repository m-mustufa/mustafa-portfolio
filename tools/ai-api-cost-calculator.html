Formulas and limits

Useful planning, without fake precision.

The calculator separates uncached input, cached input and output tokens, then combines them with your pricing and overhead. It does not pretend a cheaper model is automatically the best model.

Provider pricing, caching rules, long-context tiers and promotional rates change. Verify the linked official page before committing a budget. Taxes, payment fees, hosting, support and model-specific tools are excluded unless you enter them.
How is monthly AI API cost calculated?

users × requests × tokens ÷ 1,000,000 × model rate. Uncached input, cached input and output are priced separately. The optional extra cost per request covers items such as search or paid provider tools.

Are these OpenAI, Claude and Gemini prices live?

No. Presets were verified on November 10, 2026 using the official OpenAI, Anthropic and Google pricing pages. Use custom rates if a provider changes them.

What does profit margin include?

Revenue minus token cost, optional extra metered cost, other variable cost per user and fixed monthly cost. It is a gross planning margin, not audited profit or accounting advice.

Why can cached input cost less?

Providers can charge a lower read rate when repeated prompt content qualifies for caching. Eligibility and write/storage charges differ by provider, so enter a realistic cache percentage or leave it at zero.

Does the calculator store my numbers?

No. It is client-side only. Your usage, pricing and margin assumptions stay in the browser.
