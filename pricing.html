Common questions

Website and app development costs

How much does a WordPress website cost?

My WordPress website package starts at $85 for up to 5 pages, including theme setup, brand customization, a contact form and basic on-page SEO. More pages, custom design, WooCommerce or advanced features increase the price, and we agree the final price before work begins.

How much does Shopify store setup cost?

Shopify store setup starts at $80. It covers theme setup and customization, products, collections, menus, payments, shipping and store settings. Your Shopify plan and any paid themes or apps are billed by Shopify separately.

How much does an AI chatbot for a website cost?

An AI chatbot for your website starts at $120. It answers visitor questions from your website content and FAQs using the OpenAI or Claude API and can collect contact details for follow-up. The AI provider’s usage fees are separate and depend on how many conversations the chatbot handles. You can estimate them with my free AI API cost calculator.

How much does a SaaS MVP or custom web app cost?

A small custom web app or SaaS MVP starts at $399 for login, user roles, a dashboard and one core feature. Products that need payments, integrations, AI features or more screens are priced after we discuss the scope. For larger AI products, see my AI SaaS development services.

What does website maintenance include?

Website maintenance starts at $50 a month and covers theme, plugin and app updates, regular backups, security checks and small content changes or fixes.
