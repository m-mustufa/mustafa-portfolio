Common questions

Hiring a freelance full-stack developer in the UAE

What can a full-stack developer build for a UAE business?

A full-stack developer can build customer portals, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, internal dashboards, secure APIs, AI features, subscription billing and integrations with existing business systems. I focus on production products where the front end, backend, database and external services need to work as one system.

Can you work with businesses in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah?

Yes. I work remotely with product teams and businesses across the UAE using scheduled calls, written technical plans, milestone reviews and shared project tools. The engagement does not depend on claiming a physical UAE office.

How much does a freelance full-stack developer cost in the UAE?

Cost depends on the product scope, integrations, security requirements and delivery schedule. I review the requirements first and then propose clear milestones or a suitable ongoing engagement. That is more reliable than quoting a generic application price without understanding the work.

Can you integrate UAE PASS or UAE e-invoicing?

I can design and implement the application architecture, API flows, identity mapping, validation, audit records and failure handling needed around these integrations. Production access and formal approval still depend on the relevant authority, accredited provider and official onboarding process.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Yes. Maintenance can cover bug fixes, monitoring, performance, security updates, dependency upgrades and planned product improvements after launch. The scope can be arranged as a defined support period or ongoing engineering work.
